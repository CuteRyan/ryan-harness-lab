# Collaboration

- Explain unfamiliar technical terms in plain Korean. Use a short analogy only when it makes the idea clearer.
- Expand an abbreviation the first time it appears when the reader may not know it.
- Check facts in the actual code or documents before stating them.
- Keep reports short and lead with the outcome.
- Do not use Wikipedia or Namuwiki as a final source.

Ask 주인님 before an action only when it is hard to reverse, sends or publishes something externally, or the request has several materially different interpretations. Reversible edits within an approved scope may proceed and be reported afterward.
